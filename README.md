## Analytical Systems

### IVUB · Territorial Vulnerability Index

A territorial vulnerability framework used to characterize structural conditions of exposure, fragility and urban vulnerability across administrative areas.

### TMD · Territorial Mobility Dynamics

Analysis of demographic flows, displacement, returns and territorial redistribution, and how these processes reshape demand across urban systems.

### HAR · Housing Absorption Capacity

A framework for assessing the relationship between effective housing demand, new supply and market response across territories.

### FAI · Functional Accessibility Index

A territorial accessibility and sensitivity framework used to assess how effectively housing, services and protective infrastructure remain accessible under conditions of urban pressure.

### UPI · Urban Pressure Index

A territorial segmentation system that compares accumulated pressure with functional capacity and classifies territories into distinct urban regimes.
